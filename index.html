With the mouse: drag a note to move it, drag its trailing edge to retime it, drag empty space to marquee-select, double-click empty space to make a note and a note to delete it. ⌥-drag a note up or down for how hard it is struck. A selected note with something in front of it grows a second grip at its leading edge: drag that out and it slides into its pitch instead of starting on it — the ramp you pull out is how long the slide takes, and dragging it back to the note's start takes it off. None of that needs a key: select a single note and the Note panel under the spiral holds the same two settings as a slider and a button. Select two or more notes and a stretch handle appears at the end of the selection — drag it to scale the passage in time. Lengths are free, but everything lands on the Resolution grid (a 1/64 by default), and Align pulls notes placed earlier onto it. Time sets how long a bar is and how it divides — it moves the bar lines and the Bar and Beat snaps, and moves no notes. On the spiral, click a slot to make or remove a note at the cursor and drag one to move it round; press a chord chip under it to build that chord on the cursor, or on the selected note if there is exactly one.
